# Ardas Samaj Kalyan NGO

**Empowering Communities, Transforming Lives**

Ardas Samaj Kalyan (ASK Trust) is a non-profit organization committed to empowering marginalized communities through education, skill development, and comprehensive support.

## Our Mission

To create a self-reliant society by empowering individuals from marginalized communities. We focus on providing essential skills, education, and support systems that enable sustainable growth and a brighter future for all.

## Our Core Programs

- **Skills for Prisoners** — Providing technical and soft skills to inmates for rehabilitation and successful reintegration into society.
- **Women's SHG Support** — Empowering women's Self-Help Groups with training and resources to foster financial independence.
- **Youth Development** — Supporting and guiding the youth towards positive pathways through mentorship and skill-building programs.
