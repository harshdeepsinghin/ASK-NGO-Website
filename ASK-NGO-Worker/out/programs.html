Community & Special Initiatives

Alongside our core programs we regularly conduct targeted initiatives responding to emerging needs, seasonal opportunities, and holistic community upliftment.

COVID Relief: Food Distribution
Distributed essential food packets to vulnerable families during the pandemic lockdowns ensuring food security.
Tree Plantation Drive
Organized community-led plantation drives promoting environmental sustainability and awareness.
Cyber Security Awareness
Workshops educating youth and community members on safe digital practices and online fraud prevention.
Traffic Awareness Campaign
Sessions and street activities to promote road safety, responsible driving, and pedestrian awareness.
Summer Camp (Children)
Holistic development camp including creativity, basic computing, life skills, and recreational activities.
Smartphone Photography Workshop
Training participants to capture impactful images using mobile devices—framing, light, storytelling.
Artisan Skill Camp
Hands-on exposure to local crafts supporting creativity, self-reliance, and cultural preservation.
बहुदेशीय शिविर (Multi-speciality Camp)
Integrated camp offering health check-ups, counseling, and access to social welfare resources.
Drugs and Cyber Awareness Campaign
A joint awareness campaign to spread the message of saying yes to cyber awareness and no to drug use.
